> Ative essa opção no Flow para conseguir gerar o efeito sonoro de rasgar o pacote.

Static camera. No camera movement. No zoom. No panning. No shaking.

The clothing item remains neatly folded inside the transparent package. The woman's
hands firmly grip both sides of the package opening. With a quick and realistic motion,
she tears the plastic package open from the center. The plastic stretches naturally
and tears realistically.

At the exact moment the package is torn open, include a clear, crisp, realistic plastic
ripping sound, perfectly synchronized with the hands and plastic movement. Include
natural plastic stretching, crinkling, and tearing sounds during the opening. The sound
should feel strong, satisfying, and impactful, creating an engaging viral unboxing
effect.

Immediately after tearing, both hands pull the torn plastic away from the garment. The
packaging is completely removed from the clothing. Pull the empty plastic out of the
frame so it no longer covers the garment. Include subtle, realistic plastic crinkling
sounds as it is removed.

The folded clothing remains centered on the surface. Without pausing, both hands grab
the garment from both sides and smoothly lift it as a single physical object. Do not
duplicate it or leave a copy on the surface. Lift the same garment toward the camera
with its front facing the camera. Slightly adjust it to showcase the front design,
print, texture, and details. Present it close to the camera briefly, with subtle,
natural fabric movement sounds synchronized to the action.

## AUDIO

* Keep audio clean, realistic, and synchronized with every action
* No music, voice, talking, artificial sound effects, or unnecessary background noise

## FINAL QUALITY

* Fast but realistic product reveal
* Natural fabric movement and gravity
* Smooth hand coordination
* Realistic interaction between hands, packaging, and clothing
* No object morphing or clipping
* No hand deformation, finger distortion, or extra fingers
* No camera movement
* Ultra-realistic TikTok Shop product reveal
* Total duration approximately 5 seconds

The entire sequence should feel quick, satisfying, and natural, like a viral fashion
unboxing reveal.
