Create a realistic overhead product showcase video using the provided image as the main
reference. Keep the camera completely fixed in a top-down position at all times.
Preserve the same carpet background, lighting, framing, clothing arrangement, feminine
hands, nails, and bracelets.

The hands should interact with only one clothing piece at a time. Choose exactly two
pieces from the arrangement. First, the hands gently pick up one piece from its place on
the bed or carpet, lift it closer to the camera to show its details clearly, softly
stretch or pull the fabric a little to demonstrate the material, hold it briefly, and
then place it back neatly in the exact same original position.

After that, the hands move to a second clothing piece and repeat the same action: pick
up only that piece, bring it closer to the camera, show its details, gently stretch the
fabric a little, hold it briefly, and place it back in the exact same place.

The movements should look elegant, smooth, controlled, and natural. The camera must
remain still throughout the video. All other clothing pieces that are not being handled
must remain in place and must not move.

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions
* No changing the arrangement
* Do not pick up two pieces at the same time
* No extra products
* No distorted hands or deformed fabric
