Create a realistic top-down product placement video using the provided opening image
as the starting frame and the provided final arrangement image as the ending reference.
Keep the camera completely fixed in an overhead view. Preserve the same carpet
background, lighting, framing, shadows, and feminine hands with the same nails and
accessories.

## ACTION

The hands must handle only one clothing piece at a time. Never pick up, hold, or place
two pieces together. Perform the action step by step:

1. Bring in one piece only.
2. Gently place or lightly toss that single piece into its exact final position.
3. Adjust it naturally and remove the hands.
4. Bring in the next single piece and place it in its correct final position.
5. Repeat until all pieces are arranged.

Place each piece separately, one after another. Never place multiple items
simultaneously, stack two pieces in the hands, or overlap placement actions. Follow a
logical order based on the final arrangement image. Start with one piece, place it
correctly, then continue until the final layout matches the reference exactly.

Movements must be smooth, natural, and slightly slow, as if carefully organizing the
products on the bed or carpet.

The final frame must match the reference arrangement exactly, including the quantity
of pieces, colors, orientation, spacing, and placement.

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions
* No morphing or teleporting items
* No extra, duplicated, or missing products
* No deformed hands
* Never pick up two pieces at the same time
