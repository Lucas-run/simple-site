Create a realistic 8-second overhead product video using the provided image as the
main reference. Keep the camera completely fixed in a top-down position. Preserve the
same carpet background, lighting, framing, feminine hands, nails, bracelets, and
clothing arrangement.

The hands should pick up only one blouse in a natural way. After picking it up, gently
stretch it a little to show the fabric, then bring it closer to the camera without
moving the camera itself. While holding the blouse near the camera, softly rub and slide
the fingers over the fabric to show its texture and material quality. The movement
should look smooth, elegant, and natural, like a product showcase.

After showing the fabric up close, keep the blouse well positioned in the hands, with
the material clearly visible. Focus only on this single blouse.

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions
* No extra products or switching pieces
* No distorted hands or deformed fabric
