Create a realistic overhead product video using the provided image as the main
reference. Keep the camera completely fixed in a top-down view. Preserve the same
carpet background, lighting, framing, hands, skin tone, nails, and exact arrangement
of the clothing pieces.

## ACTION

Focus only on the top/front piece, the first visible piece on top. The hands gently
pick up only this piece and hold it from both sides, showing the fabric clearly. Then
the hands softly stretch the fabric outward in a natural way, not too hard, just enough
to show its material and elasticity. Hold briefly, relax slightly, and stretch the same
piece again. Repeat smoothly throughout the video: stretch, hold, relax a little, and
stretch again.

Only this single clothing piece may move. All pieces underneath or behind it must remain
in the same position throughout and must not be touched, removed, or rearranged.

The motion must be slow, controlled, and natural, like a demonstration of fabric
quality. Keep the same hands visible throughout the video. No sudden movements.

If needed, end with the piece still held neatly in front of the other items, maintaining
a clean product-showcase look.

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions
* No changing clothes, extra products, or switching pieces
* No distorted hands or deformed fabric
* No movement in the other garments
