Create a realistic overhead product showcase video using the provided image as the
main reference. Keep the camera completely fixed in a top-down position, directly
overhead. Preserve exactly the same bed or fabric surface, lighting, framing, hands,
nails, and all clothing pieces as they appear in the image.

## PRODUCT PRESENTATION

The clothes must remain lying on the bed throughout the video. The hands must not lift
the clothing excessively; they should gently touch and handle the pieces while keeping
them mostly resting on the bed.

The hands must interact with only one piece at a time and may present only one or, at
most, two products during the video.

For the first product, the hands gently hold the garment and slightly open it to reveal
its details. If it has straps, gently lift them. If it has a neckline, gently open the
area to show it. If needed, slightly spread the fabric to reveal the shape, stitching,
cut, texture, or design. Keep the garment mostly in the same position on the bed.

The hands may gently pull a small area of fabric, smooth it with the fingers, slightly
open the front area, and show important details naturally and elegantly. The piece must
never be completely removed from the bed or lifted too high.

After presenting the first piece, return it to its original position. If presenting a
second product, repeat the same actions: gently touch it, slightly open or spread it,
show details such as straps, neckline, cut, or fabric texture, and keep it mostly on the
bed. All other garments must remain completely still and untouched.

Movements must be smooth, slow, elegant, and natural, like a detailed product
presentation.

## FINAL CAMERA-COVER TRANSITION

Begin this action only after the product presentation is completely finished. One hand
begins a continuous movement toward the camera lens, as if reaching out to touch the
viewer.

* Gently rotate until the palm faces the camera through natural arm/forearm rotation;
  do not forcibly bend or twist the wrist
* Move in a straight line along the lens axis, approaching the camera frontally
* Maintain a constant, controlled speed that is fluid from beginning to end
* Progressively move the palm closer until it fills 100% of the frame, with no gaps
  around the edges
* Allow slight natural proximity blur in the final frames before full coverage

The camera must remain completely covered by the left palm until the cut. After the
lens is covered, do not move the hand away, open the fingers, or reveal any part of the
scene. The final frame must show only the palm, filling 100% of the frame.

Suggested duration for the camera-cover movement: approximately 1 to 1.5 seconds.

## IMPORTANT

* Complete the normal product presentation before covering the camera
* Cover the camera only at the very end; do not interrupt the presentation
* No sudden or aggressive hand movements
* Do not twist the wrist or deform the fingers
* No extra fingers or deformed hands
* The palm must completely fill the camera view in the final frame

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions before the final camera-cover action
* No fast movements or camera shake
* Do not remove clothes from the bed
* Do not deform hands or fabric
* Do not handle multiple pieces at the same time

## FINAL RESULT

A realistic continuous POV product showcase in which the hands naturally demonstrate
the clothing, then, only at the very end, one hand smoothly approaches the lens and
completely covers the camera with the palm, ending with the view fully covered for a
clean transition into the next scene.
