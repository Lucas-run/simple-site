Create a realistic overhead closing video using the provided image as the starting
reference. Keep the camera completely fixed in a top-down position. Preserve the same
carpet background, lighting, framing, feminine hands, nails, bracelets, and exact
clothing arrangement.

The video starts with all clothing pieces already arranged in place. The hands then
remove the pieces one by one, not all at once. Each time, pick up only one piece, pull
it smoothly out of the scene to the side, and return the hands to remove the next one.

## STEP-BY-STEP ACTION

1. Pick up one piece only.
2. Remove it from the frame.
3. Return the hands.
4. Pick up the next piece only.
5. Remove it from the frame.
6. Repeat until all pieces are gone.

The movements should be smooth, elegant, controlled, and slightly slow. Never remove
two pieces together or drag all the pieces at once. Each item must leave the scene
separately. All other pieces must remain in place until it is their turn to be removed.
The hands should look natural and consistent throughout.

By the end of the video, all clothing pieces must be removed from the frame, leaving
only the empty background visible for a clean closing shot.

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions
* No extra products
* No distorted hands or deformed fabric
* Do not remove multiple pieces at once
