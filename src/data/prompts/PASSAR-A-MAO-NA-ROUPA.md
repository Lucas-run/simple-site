Create a realistic overhead product showcase video using the provided image as the main
reference. Keep the camera completely fixed in a top-down position. Preserve the same
bed or fabric surface, lighting, framing, hands, nails, and all clothing pieces exactly
as they appear.

The clothes must remain lying on the bed throughout the video. The hands should not
lift the clothing too much; instead, gently touch and handle the pieces while they stay
mostly resting on the bed.

The hands should interact with only one piece at a time and may show one or at most two
products during the video.

For the first product, the hands gently hold the garment and open it slightly to reveal
its details. If it has straps, lightly lift and show them. If it has a neckline, gently
open and show it. If needed, slightly spread the fabric to show its shape, stitching,
cut, texture, or design. The motion should feel like a seller carefully presenting the
product details while keeping it mostly in place on the bed.

The hands may softly pull a small area of fabric, smooth it with the fingers, lightly
open the front area, and show important details naturally and elegantly. The piece
should never be fully removed from the bed or lifted too high.

After showing the first piece, place it back exactly as it was. If showing a second
product, repeat the same actions: gently touch it, slightly open or spread it, show
details such as straps, neckline, cut, or fabric texture, and keep it mostly resting on
the bed. The other garments must remain still and untouched.

The motion should be smooth, slow, elegant, and natural, like a product-detail
presentation video.

## EXCLUSIONS

* No camera movement, zoom, cuts, or transitions
* No fast motion or removing clothing from the bed
* No shaking
* No distorted hands or deformed fabric
* Do not handle multiple pieces at the same time
